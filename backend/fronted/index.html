WHAT IS DIGITAL ARREST?

Recognize the signs.
Respond with confidence.

Digital-arrest scams involve criminals impersonating police officers, CBI officials, cyber-crime officials, banks or other authorities and using fear or urgency to pressure people.

SCAM CHECKER

Check a suspicious interaction

Answer all eight questions. The answers will be checked by the FastAPI backend.

1. Who did the caller claim to be?

2. Did the caller threaten you with arrest?

3. Did they demand money or payment?

4. Did they ask for OTP, bank details or sensitive information?

5. Did they force you to stay on a video call?

6. Did they create urgency or tell you not to disconnect?

7. Did they ask you to install an unknown app?

8. Did they claim to represent an official authority?

🚩 Common Red Flags

  • Threat of immediate arrest
  • Impersonation of authorities
  • Demand for money
  • Requests for OTP or bank details
  • Forced video call
  • Unknown app installation
  • Pressure to act immediately
